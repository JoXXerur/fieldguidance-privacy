Common Support Topics

GPS & Location

FieldGuidance uses device location services for live guidance, field boundary recording, and fieldwork tracking. Check that location access is enabled in your device's Settings. GPS accuracy depends on device capabilities, reception, and operating conditions.

Field Mapping

Create field boundaries by drawing on a map, recording with GPS, or importing supported KML/KMZ files.

Cultivation Planning

Configure your implement dimensions and plan cultivation paths based on your field and intended working method.

Data & Backups

Use the app's backup and restore tools to manage copies of your FieldGuidance information. Keep exported backups in a location you control.

Privacy

Learn how FieldGuidance handles location data, field information, and app settings in our Privacy Policy.
