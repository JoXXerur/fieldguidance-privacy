Privacy Policy

FieldGuidance is a mobile application for field mapping, cultivation planning, GPS guidance, and agricultural fieldwork tracking. This policy explains how information is handled when you use FieldGuidance on your iPhone or iPad.

Effective date: 2026-11-09

1. Information Used by the App

FieldGuidance processes information necessary to provide its features, including:

  • Location data: GPS coordinates, movement, speed, and heading used for field mapping, live guidance, and recording cultivation activity.
  • Field information: Field boundaries, imported geographic data, cultivation plans, and fieldwork history.
  • Equipment information: Implement dimensions, offsets, and related configuration settings.
  • App preferences: Settings and preferences used to customize the application.

This information is used to provide the functionality requested by the user.

2. Location Services

FieldGuidance requests access to your device's location services to provide GPS guidance, record field boundaries, and track agricultural operations.

Location access is used for these purposes, not for advertising or profiling.

You can manage location permissions at any time through your device's Settings. Disabling location access may prevent GPS-dependent features from functioning.

3. Data Storage

FieldGuidance stores field information, cultivation plans, GPS recordings, and application settings locally on your device.

FieldGuidance does not upload these records to developer-operated servers.

You control any files you explicitly export or share. Depending on your device settings, locally stored app data may also be included in backups managed by Apple.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

FieldGuidance does not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

The app may use Apple platform services, including location and mapping services, to provide its functionality. Information processed independently by Apple is subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies.

Information you explicitly export or share is handled by the destination or service you choose.

5. Analytics and Advertising

FieldGuidance does not include third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or developer-operated analytics services.

We do not use your location history or agricultural field data for marketing, profiling, or targeted advertising.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application.

You can withdraw location permission through your device's Settings.

Removing the application deletes its locally stored app data, subject to any separate device backups or exported copies you have retained.

If you contact us for support, we may retain your correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and fulfill applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

FieldGuidance relies on the security protections provided by iOS and iPadOS to help protect locally stored application data.

We recommend keeping your device updated and using its built-in security features.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information processed by us.

For information stored only on your device, you generally manage that information directly.

If you have contacted us for support, you may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information associated with that correspondence, subject to applicable law.

Users in the European Economic Area may also contact their relevant data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

FieldGuidance is designed for agricultural fieldwork and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to FieldGuidance, its features, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL, with an updated effective date where appropriate.
